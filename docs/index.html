AURA

Adaptive Uncertainty-Aware Robotic Autonomy

A ROS 2 research platform for autonomous robots that monitor the reliability of their own sensing and state estimation and adapt before uncertainty becomes failure.

AURA Modules

Health Monitoring

Monitors LiDAR quality, odometry consistency, state-estimation reliability and localization uncertainty.

Fault Injection

Introduces controlled LiDAR, IMU, odometry and perception faults for repeatable experiments.

Adaptive Control

Changes navigation behavior according to the current health of the autonomy stack.

Reinforcement Learning

Uses PPO to learn adaptive behavior under sensor degradation and compound faults.

Research Question

Can an autonomous robot detect when its own sensing and state-estimation systems become unreliable and adapt early enough to maintain safe navigation?
