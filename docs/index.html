AURA

Adaptive Uncertainty-Aware Robotic Autonomy

A ROS 2 research platform exploring how autonomous robots can detect degraded sensing, perception and localization, then adapt before uncertainty becomes failure.

AURA Modules

Health Monitoring

Monitors sensor freshness, state-estimation disagreement and localization reliability.

Fault Injection

Introduces controlled LiDAR, IMU, odometry and perception failures.

Adaptive Control

Changes robot behavior according to the health of the autonomy stack.

Reinforcement Learning

Uses PPO to learn when to trade speed, accuracy and compute cost for safety.

Research Goal

Can an autonomous robot detect when its own sensing and state-estimation systems become unreliable and adapt early enough to continue navigating safely?
